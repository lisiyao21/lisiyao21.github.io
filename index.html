Li Siyao 李思尧

Siyao recently completed his PhD in MMLab of Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, supervised by Prof. Chen Change Loy and Prof. Ziwei Liu.
He is currently a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs in Redmond, USA, where he works on HumanCLAW, a closed-loop framework and benchmark for vision-language models acting through a humanoid body.
From August 2024 to March 2025, he visited the Perceiving Systems Group of Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Tuebingen, Germany, where he was supervised by Prof. Michael J. Black and worked closely with Yao Feng and Omid Taheri.
Before his PhD, he was a full-time researcher in SenseTime Research, working closely with Quan Wang, Wenxiu Sun and Chao Dong.
His current interests focus on developing intelligent embodied agent to interact with open, dynamic worlds. He is an awardee of 2024 Google PhD Fellowship.

I am currently on the job market and seeking full-time opportunities. Please feel free to contact me if you think my background is a good match.
